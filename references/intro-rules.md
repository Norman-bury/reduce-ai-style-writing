# Introduction, Motivation, Gap, and Contribution Rules

Use these rules for introduction paragraphs that explain background, motivation, research gaps, and contribution positioning. These paragraphs often keep a high AI rate if they retain clean academic skeletons such as `已有研究指出`, `能否`, `直接影响`, `重要方向`, and `主要贡献在于`.

| High AI-risk expression | Lower-template direction |
|---|---|
| 正在从 A 转向 B | 正在由 A 逐渐转到 B |
| 持续产生网络流量 | 一直有网络流量产生 |
| 这些流量既反映 A，也可能暴露 B | 流量既可以显示 A，也可以显示 B |
| 已有研究指出 / 表明 | 已有研究显示 / 研究发现 |

| 内存开销 | 内存占用 |

| 在这种场景下 | 此时的 / 在这种情况下 |
| 已经不是单纯的 A | 不再只是个 A / 不只是 A |

## Micro-Pattern Notes

- Change list connectors carefully: `与` can often become `和` or `以及`, `或` can become `或者`, and `则` can become `就` or be removed.
- Add small explanatory words only when they fit the sentence: `所处`, `所拥有`, `所带来的`, `等`, `也就`, `就`, and `又` can lower the polished skeleton without changing facts.
- A low-AI sample may contain grammar damage. Keep the useful pattern but repair it. For example, write `由于客户端分布的不同，会造成局部更新漂移、全局模型不稳定等问题`, not a sentence fragment.
- Do not overuse the same downgrade. If every sentence uses `可以`, `就是`, or `问题`, the paragraph becomes low quality even if the detector score improves.

## Guardrails

- Keep abbreviations such as IIoT, IDS, FL-IDS, XAI-IDS, FedAvg, FedProx, SHAP, CNN, LSTM, GRU, and 1D-CNN unless the user provides a style that deliberately removes spaces.
- Do not introduce ungrammatical fragments just to mimic a detector-passing sample.
- Keep citations attached to the same claims.
- If a sample's low-AI rewrite has grammar damage, preserve the useful word-level pattern but repair the sentence.
